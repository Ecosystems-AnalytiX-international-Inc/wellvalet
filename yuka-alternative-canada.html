Why WellValet is the Premier Yuka Alternative in Canada

1. Robust Canadian Grocery Barcode Coverage

Canadian supermarkets like Loblaws, Real Canadian Superstore, Metro, Sobeys, Farm Boy, and No Frills carry thousands of private label items and Canadian-specific UPC barcodes. WellValet indexes 3 Million+ global products with active Canadian catalog updates so you rarely hit an "unknown product" error.

2. OCR Ingredient Label Scanning (No Barcode Required)

Ever encountered an imported product, bakery item, or specialty store brand without a standard barcode? WellValet includes an advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR) camera tool that reads the physical ingredients list on the packaging and instantly analyzes every ingredient for safety and allergens.

3. PIPEDA Compliance & True Data Privacy

Your dietary preferences, age, weight, and allergy profiles are sensitive personal information. Under Canadian law (PIPEDA), WellValet stores your personal health profile securely on your local device. We never sell user data, track you across the web, or display third-party advertisements.

4. Personalised Wellness Scores, Not One-Size-Fits-All

A product that is healthy for an endurance athlete might not suit someone managing hypertension or peanut allergies. WellValet customizes product scoring to your specific age, body goals, dietary restrictions, and allergy profiles.
