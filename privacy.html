3. Personal Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: email address, password (stored as a salted hash; we never store plaintext passwords), and display name.
  • Wellness and product-preference profile (optional): age range, general weight range, wellness goals, dietary preferences, product-sensitivity preferences, ingredient exclusions or avoidance preferences. This information is voluntary and used solely to personalize product scores. You may use the core Services without completing a wellness profile.
  • Shopping lists: items you add to your personal or family shopping list.
  • Family group data: family group name, your display name, your role (admin or member), and shopping list items you contribute.
  • Communications: messages you send to us and prompts you submit to the AI Valet assistant. You must not include PHI, medical records, health records, diagnoses, lab results, prescriptions, clinician notes, or other regulated health-care information.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Scan history: barcodes you scan, products matched, resulting scores, and timestamps.
  • Usage and technical data: device type, operating system version, app version, language settings, crash logs and diagnostic data, and IP address.
  • On-device data: authentication token, subscription status, personal shopping list, and wellness profile cache — stored locally on your device.

3.3 Information from Third Parties

Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat may confirm the status of your subscription, in-app purchase, entitlement, cancellation, or refund. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

3.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise geolocation data
  • Contact lists, photos, or browsing history outside the app
  • Camera frames (processed in real time for barcode scanning only; not stored on our servers)
  • Protected health information (PHI), medical records, diagnoses, lab results, prescriptions, or other regulated health-care information — you must not submit this to the Services

4. Why We Collect and Use Personal Information

  • To generate personalized wellness scores and product information based on your wellness and product-preference profile
  • To provide optional ingredient, product-sensitivity, and dietary-preference flagging
  • To operate account authentication, family-group administration, customer support, and verification
  • To process and manage subscriptions, free trials, plan changes, and payment-related records
  • To process AI Valet prompts and generate responses
  • To maintain, improve and measure security, integrity, and performance of the Services
  • To comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our Terms
We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioural advertising. The app does not display third-party ads.

5. Consent

Express consent is obtained for the collection of optional wellness and product-preference information, for marketing communications, and at sign-up via acceptance of this Policy and the Terms. You may withdraw consent where legally available, but doing so may limit access to features that require the information.

Family Plan notice: If you join a family group, your display name and shopping items are visible to the family administrator. The administrator does not gain access to your wellness profile, scan history, or account credentials.

6. Artificial Intelligence (AI Valet) Processing

The AI Valet feature is powered by a third-party large language model provided by Anthropic, PBC. When you use AI Valet, the text of your request and limited relevant context (such as your current shopping list items and applicable wellness preferences) may be transmitted to Anthropic and its sub-processors, whose servers may be located in the United States or other jurisdictions.

We send only the minimum prompt content and context required to provide the requested response. We do not send your password, payment information, full account record, or prohibited health-care information unless you include it in a prompt contrary to this Policy.

Important: AI-generated scores, recommendations, and AI Valet responses are informational and wellness-oriented only. They must not be relied upon as allergy-safety guidance or medical, clinical, dietetic, or emergency advice.

9. Retention and Deletion

  • Account data: retained while your account is active and deleted or de-identified within 90 days of verified account deletion, except where required by law
  • Wellness and product-preference profile: retained until you edit or delete it, or your account is deleted
  • Scan history and shopping-list data: retained while your account is active or until you delete the applicable items
  • Aggregated or anonymized data: may be retained for analytics, service improvement, and product development where it does not identify you

You can request account deletion in-app or view our Account Deletion Page for complete instructions. Account deletion requests are actioned within 30 days of verification, and data is fully deleted or de-identified within 90 days. You may also email support@wellvalet.com (subject: "Account Deletion Request").

10. Safeguards

  • Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and encryption at rest for our database
  • Password hashing with a modern salted algorithm; we cannot read your password
  • Token-based authentication with expiring credentials
  • Restricted production access, least-privilege roles, staff confidentiality obligations, and logging and monitoring
