ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your account

WellValet · Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc.

You can delete your WellValet account and the personal data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do it — use the first if you still have the app installed, because it is immediate.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

1Open WellValet and sign in.
2Go to Profile.
3Scroll to Delete Account at the bottom of the page.
4Confirm twice. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on that device.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Use this if you have already uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or would simply prefer not to install it again.

Email a deletion request

Send the request from the email address on your WellValet account so we can verify it is you. If you email from a different address we will not be able to action the request.

Request Account Deletion

support@wellvalet.com · subject: “Account Deletion Request”

How long it takes

In-app deletion (Option 1) takes effect immediately. Email requests (Option 2) are actioned within 30 days of us verifying the request. In both cases your data is fully deleted or de-identified within 90 days, except for the records listed above that we are legally required to keep. This matches section 9 of our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by WellValet, so we cannot cancel them for you. Cancel yours before deleting your account, or you will continue to be charged:

  • iPhone / iPad: App Store → Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play → Subscriptions

Before you delete

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We cannot restore a deleted account, even if you sign up again with the same email address.
  • Any unused portion of a subscription period is not refunded by WellValet. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.
  • If you only want to stop paying, cancel the subscription and keep the free account — you do not need to delete anything.

Questions

If you are unsure about any of the above, email support@wellvalet.com before submitting a deletion request. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days. See also our Support page and Privacy Policy.
