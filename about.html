Our Story & Mission

WellValet was born out of a simple frustration: grocery shopping shouldn't require a degree in biochemistry to know if a product is healthy or safe for your family.

Navigating Canadian supermarket aisles — from national chains like Loblaws, Metro, and Sobeys to local neighborhood markets — means encountering thousands of complex packaged food labels, confusing marketing claims, and hidden allergens. Most existing scanning apps were either built for overseas markets or monetized by advertising sponsors.

Developed by Ecosystem & AnalytiX International Inc. in Toronto, Ontario, WellValet was engineered from day one to serve Canadian shoppers with transparent, un-biased product insights tailored to personal health profiles.

Our Core Guiding Principles

🛡️

PIPEDA Privacy First

Your dietary preferences, age, weight, and allergy restrictions belong to you. WellValet complies with Canadian PIPEDA regulations — your data stays private on your device.

🚫

100% Zero Advertising

No sponsored products, no paid brand rankings, and no pop-up ads. Ever. Our product wellness scores are calculated strictly from objective nutrition and ingredient data.

🇨🇦

Canadian Supermarket Depth

Over 3 Million global and local products indexed, with specialized database coverage for Canadian store brands, private labels, and regional food distributors.

📷

Smart OCR Label AI

Not limited to barcodes — point your camera at any physical ingredient list or bakery packaging, and our OCR engine analyzes every component in seconds.
