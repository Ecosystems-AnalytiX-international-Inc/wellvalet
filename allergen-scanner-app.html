WellValet solves this by acting as your personal food safety valet. Point your phone camera at any food product barcode or physical ingredient label, and receive an instant personalized allergen alert tailored to your health profile.

How WellValet's Allergen Scanner Works

1. Create Your Private Profile: Set your age, weight, health targets, and specific allergen restrictions once. Your valet remembers everything locally on your phone.

2. Instant Barcode Scanning: Point your camera at any barcode in Loblaws, Superstore, Metro, Sobeys, or Walmart. WellValet checks the product formulation against your exact allergy profile.

3. OCR Ingredient Camera Tool: For unbarcoded bakery items, local artisanal goods, or imported foods, use WellValet's OCR scanner. Take a photo of the ingredient list text and let AI analyze every component in seconds.

4. Clear Safety Badges: Receive immediate visual feedback — ✅ Great choice, ⚠️ Moderate caution, or 🚨 Avoid (Allergen Detected) with a clear breakdown of why.

Why Privacy Matters for Allergy & Health Data

Unlike ad-supported apps that track consumer health data for target marketing, WellValet is 100% ad-free and PIPEDA compliant. Your health restrictions, dietary needs, and purchase history stay strictly private on your device.
